# Refunds
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 14 days of approval. Opened digital downloads are not eligible for a refund unless the file is faulty.

# Shipping
Shipping is free on orders over 50 dollars. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days, and express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days for an extra fee. Every order ships with a tracking number.

# Returns
Customers return physical items by post within 30 days of delivery. Returned items must be unused and in their original packaging, and return postage is free for faulty items.

# Warranty
Electronics carry a one year warranty against manufacturing faults. The warranty covers repair or replacement, but not accidental damage, and claims need proof of purchase.

# Support
Customer support answers email within one business day. Live chat is open on weekdays from 9am to 6pm, and urgent order problems can be raised by phone.
